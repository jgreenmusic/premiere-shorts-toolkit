# Privacy Policy — Shorts Toolkit / Poosic Post Studio

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

Shorts Toolkit (with its built-in Post Studio) is a desktop app that runs on your own Windows PC.

**What it collects:** nothing. There is no server, no account with us, no analytics and no tracking.

**What stays on your PC:** your videos, captions, written posts, settings, and the login tokens for
the platforms you connect (YouTube, TikTok and others). They are stored in your Windows user folder
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\PostStudio` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ShortsToolkit`) and never sent to us.

**What goes to a platform:** only when you connect an account and send a video. The video file and
the text you wrote are sent directly from your PC to that platform (for example TikTok or YouTube),
under that platform's own privacy policy. From TikTok the app only reads your basic profile
(display name, avatar, open id) to show which account is connected, and uploads videos you choose
to your TikTok drafts.

**AI writing:** titles and captions are written by an AI model running locally on your PC (Ollama).
Your videos and transcripts are not sent to any AI service.

**Removing your data:** disconnect an account in the app (Publish > Accounts), or delete the folders
above. You can also revoke the app's access in your TikTok / Google account settings at any time.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/jgreenmusic/premiere-shorts-toolkit/issues
